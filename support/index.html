Frequently asked questions

How do the three lines work?

Calc shows three lines: Net (before tax), Tax and Gross (what you pay). Tap any line and type on the keypad: that line is the input, and the other two update as you type. There is no Calculate button and no add or remove switch — to take the tax out of a receipt total, type the total into Gross; to add tax, type into Net; to find the price from a tax amount, type into Tax. Tap a number to copy it, or use Copy all for the three lines as text.

Why is Net + Tax always exactly Gross?

Taxpad works out two of the three amounts and takes the third from the rounded pair, so the lines on screen always add up to the cent, whichever line you type into. Amounts are rounded half up to the minor unit of your currency (cents, pence) only when they are shown and written to the tape; Settings › Rounding can round to whole units instead. With a rate of 0 % the Tax line takes no input.

Where do the rates come from?

The presets are standard rates for the US states (state base rates), the Canadian provinces, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Australia, New Zealand, the European Union, Switzerland and Norway. Each group was checked against the source it names, on the day shown in the table below and in the Rates tab. On first launch the rates of your device's region become chips on Calc. The presets ship with the app and are checked again before every update — Taxpad never downloads rates — so Settings › Rates › Check for rate updates opens this table: if a rate changed after your version was released, edit it in Rates. Your edits are kept when an update brings new presets.

For information only, not tax advice. Verify rates with your tax authority.

Why are there no local US rates by ZIP code?

Local sales tax rates in the US change hundreds of times a year and differ between neighbouring streets, so a list in the app would soon be wrong. The US presets are the state base rates. Add your city's combined rate as a rate of your own (for example "Houston 8.25 %"); Taxpad doesn't use your location.

How do I add or edit a rate, or stack two of them?

In the Rates tab, tap + Custom (or + on Calc). Give the rate a name, type the rate and, if you like, its source, and keep Show on Calc on to see it as a chip. A rate can stack up to three components, such as GST 5 % + PST 7 %: each component is worked out from the same net amount, and the Tax line shows the breakdown. Edit or delete a rate in Rates; drag to change the order of the chips. To find a rate from two amounts, hold a chip and choose Solve rate from net & gross.

In which order are a discount and the tax applied?

In Percent › Discount the discount comes first and the tax is charged on the discounted price, the way shops ring up a sale. For a coupon where the tax is still charged on the original price, switch on Tax on original price. The tape shows which of the two a calculation used, so two discounts are never confused.

What is the difference between margin and markup?

Percent › Margin shows both at once from a cost and a price: margin is the profit as a share of the price, markup the profit as a share of the cost. A cost of 80 and a price of 100 is a 20 % margin and a 25 % markup. Set margin finds the price for a margin you want.

What is free, and what does the unlock add?

Free, for good: the whole calculator, every preset, Percent, the widgets, the Control Center control, the shortcuts, the last 10 calculations on the tape and up to three rates of your own (presets you adopt don't count). Unlock everything is a one-time purchase that shows the whole tape with its CSV export and lets you keep more than three rates of your own. Nothing is ever deleted: on the free tape, older calculations are folded under one line until you unlock. There is no subscription and no ads; the App Store shows the price in your currency before you buy.

To restore the purchase on a new iPhone or iPad, sign in with the same Apple Account and tap Restore Purchases in Settings › Purchase or on the unlock screen.

How do I export or share the tape?

The Tape tab lists every calculation by day; tap one to bring it back to Calc, swipe to delete it. Share as text sends the lines as plain text. Export CSV (also in Settings › Data) writes a file for Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets with the columns date, mode, rate name, rate %, net, tax, gross and currency. The file is UTF-8 with lines ending in CRLF; in English it uses commas between fields and a decimal point, in languages that write decimals with a comma (German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Russian, Portuguese) semicolons between fields and a decimal comma, so the spreadsheet reads the numbers as numbers. Without the unlock, the export covers a tape of up to 10 calculations.

What do the widgets, the control and the shortcuts do?

The small widget on the Home Screen shows a pinned rate and your last result; the Lock Screen widget shows the same; a tap opens Calc. The Control Center control opens Calc with the cursor in the Tax line. In the Shortcuts app and with Siri you can add tax, find the price before tax and take a percent of a number without opening Taxpad.

How do I delete my data?

Swipe a calculation on the tape to delete it, or use Settings › Data › Delete All Calculations; your rates and settings stay. Delete your own rates in the Rates tab. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. We hold no copy, because nothing is ever sent to us.

Which devices and languages are supported?

Taxpad runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later; on a wide iPad window the tape sits beside Calc. It is in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Dutch, Polish and Russian. Amounts follow your device's region, and the currency can be changed in Settings › Currency. It works fully offline.
