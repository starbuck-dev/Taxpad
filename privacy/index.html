Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Summary: Taxpad collects no data. There is no account, no server of ours, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your calculations, your rates and your settings stay on your device, and whatever you copy, share or export goes only where you send it.

1. Who we are

Taxpad is an iPhone and iPad app provided by Sergei Stasenko (“we”).

2. Data we do not collect

We don't collect, receive or store any personal data through the app. Taxpad has no sign-in and no account, we run no server for it, and it contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or other third-party SDKs. The app doesn't track you across apps or websites, doesn't use the advertising identifier and never asks for tracking permission. It asks for no permissions at all and sends no notifications.

3. Your calculations and rates

The tape of your calculations, your own rates, the chips you pin to Calc and your settings (currency, rounding) are stored on your device only, in the app's own storage. The rate presets are part of the app; Taxpad never downloads anything. We can't see, read or recover any of it.

Like other app data, Taxpad's data is included in your device backups if you use iCloud Backup or back up to your computer. Those backups belong to your Apple Account or your computer, and we have no access to them. Taxpad doesn't sync your data between devices.

4. Widgets, Control Center and shortcuts

The widgets read your pinned rate and your last result from a file the app keeps in a shared folder on your device; that file never leaves the device. The Control Center control only opens the app. The shortcuts you run in the Shortcuts app or with Siri work out their result on your device; how Siri handles what you say is described in Apple's privacy policy.

5. Copying, sharing and exports

A number is copied to the clipboard only when you tap it. When you share the tape as text or export a CSV file, the text or file is created on your device and goes only where you send it with the share sheet (Mail, Messages, Files, AirDrop or another app). A preset's source and the pages of this website open in your browser only when you tap them. These transfers are handled by Apple or by the app you choose, not by us.

6. Purchases

The optional Unlock everything purchase is made through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, your Apple Account or your payment details. The app asks the App Store only whether the unlock has been bought on your Apple Account. Apple's handling of this data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

7. Ratings

Once, after your third calculation on the tape, the app may show Apple's standard rating prompt. Whether and how you rate is handled entirely by Apple; the app only keeps, on your device, how many calculations you have made and whether it has asked.

8. Emails to support

If you write to us, we receive your email address and what you send, including the app version, iOS version, device, region and currency that the in-app support email adds. We use this only to answer you, don't share it, and delete it when it is no longer needed for your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR.

9. Deleting your data

You can delete single calculations, the whole tape with Settings › Data › Delete All Calculations, and your own rates in the Rates tab. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request, apart from emails you have sent us.

10. Children

Taxpad is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to access, correct and delete personal data about you and to complain to a data protection authority. Since the app collects no data, these rights concern only emails you have sent us; write to ssx51f@gmail.com.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever starts to collect data, the policy will say so before that version is released.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, write to ssx51f@gmail.com.
